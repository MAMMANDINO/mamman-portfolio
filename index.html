E-Commerce Delivery Performance Dashboard

Project Name: RoutePulse

Evaluating delivery performance and the gap between current delivery metrics and target goals.

Business Problem: Tracking delivery timelines across orders to see where fulfillment meets or misses delivery targets.
Data Analyzed: 100,000 orders.
Analysis & Findings: Measured performance against a 90% on-time delivery goal. Findings showed 74.4% on-time delivery (24,000 late orders out of 100,000), an average delivery time of 5.0 days, and an average customer rating of 4.21.
Decision Support: Helps teams identify the size of the delivery performance gap and focus attention on service targets.

Reviewing commercial performance, sales distribution, and customer order outcomes.

CASE STUDY

Logistics & Fulfillment Performance Dashboard

Project Name: NovaCart / NOVAOPS

Tracing order flow: Order → Processing → Picking & Packing → Dispatch → Delivery.

Business Problem: Identifying where delays occur across warehouse fulfillment and shipping carriers.
Data Analyzed: 100,000 fulfillment records (20,000 customers, 500 products, 4 warehouses, 6 carriers).
Analysis & Findings: Tracked order statuses (65,747 on time, 18,267 late, 15,978 in transit). Recorded late-order causes: Carrier Delay (5,687), Warehouse Backlog (4,360), Picking (2,633), Weather (1,637), Address (1,592), Customer Unavailable (1,485), and Packing (873).
Decision Support: Highlights specific operational bottlenecks in warehouse tasks and carrier handoffs.

E-Commerce Inventory Replenishment & Stockout Control System

Inventory-control analysis modeling stock levels and replenishment needs.

Business Problem: Monitoring stock levels across a product catalog to maintain item availability.
Data Analyzed: 199,946 sales rows covering 1,000 SKUs and 75 suppliers (997 SKUs sold).
Method & Findings: Used Days Cover and Projected Stock After Lead Time calculations via Power Query. Identified that 26 SKUs were below 10 units.

Manufacturing Telemetry Downtime Analysis

Context: Deloitte Australia Virtual Job Simulation — Forage

Analyzing equipment sensor logs to identify primary causes of industrial downtime.

Business Problem: Identifying equipment failure patterns and downtime concentration across heavy machinery.
